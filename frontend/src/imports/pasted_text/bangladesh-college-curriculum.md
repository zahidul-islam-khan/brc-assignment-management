# IMPORTANT: Use Bangladeshi College/Higher Secondary Curriculum

The system is intended for a **school/college environment**, so do NOT use university-level CSE subjects such as Database Management Systems, Data Structures, Operating Systems, Computer Networks, Software Engineering, etc.

Replace the existing CSE/university-oriented dummy data throughout the entire Figma design with realistic **Bangladeshi college / Higher Secondary (HSC) curriculum data**.

The academic structure should be organized by the three major groups:

1. Science
2. Business Studies (Commerce)
3. Humanities (Arts)

Use realistic Bangladeshi college names, student names, teachers, classes, subjects, assignments, and academic information.
